
# Take-Home Assignment: Customer Churn Prediction

## Overview

Thank you for taking the time to complete this take-home exercise. This assignment is designed to evaluate your ability to:

- Frame a business problem analytically  
- Apply sound data science methodology  
- Build and evaluate predictive models  
- Communicate insights and trade-offs effectively  

You will work with a **synthetic customer churn dataset** (provided separately) and develop a **binary classification model** to predict churn.



## Business Context

Customer churn is a critical driver of revenue loss in subscription-based and recurring-revenue businesses. Proactively identifying customers at risk of churning allows the business to:

- Target retention campaigns  
- Allocate marketing spend efficiently  
- Improve customer lifetime value  

In this assignment, you are tasked with developing a model that predicts whether a customer will churn within a defined time window.



## Problem Definition

Using the provided dataset:

- The **target variable** represents whether a customer has churned (1) or not (0)
- You will build a **binary classification model** to predict churn probability

You should approach this as if you are solving a real-world business problem, not just a modeling exercise.


## Your Task

### 1. Data Exploration & Understanding

- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA)

### 2. Data Preparation

- Clean and preprocess the data

### 3. Modeling

- Build at least one binary classification model. You are encouraged to compare multiple approaches.


### 4. Model Evaluation

- Evaluate model performance using appropriate metrics. 

### 5. Insights & Recommendations

- Identify key drivers of churn  
- Provide actionable recommendations


## Deliverables

### A. Code

Submit your working notebook or codebase (Python).

- Should be clear and readable  
- Include comments where necessary

Prepare a **concise presentation (10–15 minutes)** covering:

1. Problem framing  
2. Data understanding & key insights  
3. Modeling approach & rationale  
4. Model performance & trade-offs  
5. Final recommendations  

**Audience:** Non-technical + technical stakeholders (e.g., Product, Marketing, Analytics leadership)
